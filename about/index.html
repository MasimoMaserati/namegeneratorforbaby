About Random Names Wheel Generator

Learn about our free tool.

Our Mission

We built this tool because picking a baby name shouldn't mean scrolling through endless alphabetical lists or getting lost in comment threads arguing about spelling variants. Random Names Wheel Generator started as a simple side project: a way to turn the overwhelming task of choosing a name into something quick, visual, and genuinely fun. Here's how it works. Add the names you're considering — or pick from one of our curated lists covering everything from classic Biblical names to modern Nordic and Japanese options — and spin the wheel. Every spin is powered by a truly random selection process, so there's no bias, no hidden weighting, and no favorites. It's just a fast, fair way to narrow down your options or settle a debate between partners, family members, or friends. While our name wheel is designed with expecting parents in mind, it's flexible enough for classroom name-drawing, baby shower games, or any situation where you need a fun, unbiased way to pick from a list.

Why people use our tool

It's free — no sign-up, no paywall, no limits on how many times you spin It's private — nothing you type is saved, stored, or sent to a server; everything runs in your browser It's fast — no clutter, no unnecessary steps, just add names and spin It's mobile-friendly — works just as well on your phone as your desktop, so you can spin the wheel wherever inspiration strikes We're a small, independent site and we're always working on adding more name categories and improving the tool based on feedback. If you have suggestions, get in touch — we'd love to hear from you.
