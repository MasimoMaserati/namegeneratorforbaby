Frequently asked questions

What should I consider when choosing a name?

Consider how the name sounds with your surname, how it is spelled and pronounced where you live, and whether its cultural background feels appropriate to your family.

Are name meanings always exact?

No. Etymologies can be debated, and meanings may change across languages, historical periods and spelling traditions. Treat short meaning notes as a starting point for further research.
