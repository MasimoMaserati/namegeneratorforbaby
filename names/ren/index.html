Ren - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Lotus · Love · Refined

Origin: Japanese

Kanji: 蓮 蓮
(The kanji 蓮 represents the lotus flower, symbolizing purity. However, it can also be written as 恋 meaning love, or 廉 meaning honest.)

Pronunciation: REN

About the Name Ren

Meaning "lotus," "love," or "refined," Ren is admired for its simplicity and elegance. It is incredibly short, consisting of just one syllable, which gives it a modern and striking feel. The lotus flower is highly revered in Japanese culture, symbolizing purity and enlightenment.

Kanji Combinations

Because Japanese names can be written in multiple ways, Ren's meaning shifts slightly depending on the characters chosen. 蓮 (lotus) is very popular for its nature connection, while 恋 (love) gives it a romantic tone. 廉 (honest/refined) is a classic choice for boys.

Popularity

Ren has been skyrocketing in popularity in Japan over the last two decades. It consistently ranks in the top 10 for boys' names. Its easy pronunciation and cool, one-syllable sound have also made it increasingly popular in Western countries.

Famous People

Ren Nagase is a famous Japanese actor and member of the popular boy band King & Prince. The name is also commonly used for fictional characters in anime and manga.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ren a unisex name?
While it is predominantly used for boys in Japan, it can occasionally be used for girls due to its soft sound.
