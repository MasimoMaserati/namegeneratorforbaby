Caoimhe - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Gentle, Beautiful, Precious

Origin: Irish (Gaelic)

Irish Spelling: Caoimhe Caoimhe
(Pronounced "KEE-va" or "KWEE-va". Derived from the Irish word "caomh," meaning gentle or precious.)

Pronunciation: KEE-va

About the Name Caoimhe

Meaning "gentle," "beautiful," or "precious," Caoimhe is a traditional Irish name that has seen a massive resurgence in popularity. It is the feminine form of the name Caoimhín (Kevin). The name has a soft, elegant sound that appeals to modern parents.

Pronunciation & Spelling

The spelling is notoriously tricky for non-Irish people. The "Caoi" makes a "Kee" sound, and the "mhe" makes a "va" sound. It is sometimes anglicized as Keeva, which is much easier for English speakers to read, but the traditional spelling is widely used in Ireland.

Popularity

Caoimhe has consistently ranked in the top 50 girls' names in Ireland over the past two decades. While it is very common in Ireland, it remains a rare and unique choice internationally.

Famous People

While there aren't many globally famous celebrities with this name, it is a very common name in Ireland. Caoimhe O'Callaghan is a known Irish journalist, and the name frequently appears in Irish literature and media.
