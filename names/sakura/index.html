Sakura - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Cherry blossom

Origin: Japanese

Kanji: 桜 桜
(The kanji 桜 represents the cherry blossom tree and its beautiful flowers, symbolizing renewal and the fleeting nature of life.)

Pronunciation: sah-KOO-rah

About the Name Sakura

Meaning "cherry blossom," Sakura is one of Japan's most iconic names. The cherry blossom symbolizes beauty, renewal, and the fleeting nature of life, making this name a timeless favorite. It evokes images of spring, nature, and elegance.

Kanji Combinations

While 桜 (meaning cherry blossom) is the most common way to write Sakura, it can also be written using different kanji combinations. For example, 咲 (bloom) and 良 (good) give the name a slightly different nuance, focusing on the act of blooming beautifully.

Popularity

Sakura has consistently ranked among the top 100 most popular names for girls in Japan. In recent years, its popularity has spread internationally, especially among parents looking for a name that represents nature and beauty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sakura a unisex name?
While historically Sakura is mostly used for girls, it can sometimes be used for boys, though it is quite rare.

What does Sakura mean in English?
It translates directly to "cherry blossom".
