Zipporah - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Bird, Birdie

Origin: Hebrew / Biblical

Hebrew Spelling: צִפּוֹרָה צִפּוֹרָה
(Derived from the Hebrew word "tsippor," which translates directly to "bird.")

Pronunciation: zi-POR-ah

About the Name Zipporah

Zipporah is a beautiful and exotic biblical name meaning "bird." In the Book of Exodus, Zipporah is a significant figure: she is the wife of Moses and the daughter of Jethro, the priest of Midian. She is known for her strength and quick action.

Spelling & Variations

The name is sometimes spelled Tzipporah or Zipora. It has a very ancient and distinct sound that stands out. In modern times, it appeals to parents looking for a rare biblical name that has not been overused.

Popularity

Zipporah is quite rare in the United States, never ranking in the top 1000. However, it is a beloved choice in Jewish communities and among Christian parents who appreciate its deep Old Testament roots.

Famous People

While not a common name for celebrities, Zipporah is a well-known historical figure. In pop culture, the name was famously given to the character Zipporah in the animated film "The Prince of Egypt."
