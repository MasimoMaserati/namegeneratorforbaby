Rebekah - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: To tie, to bind, captivating

Origin: Hebrew / Biblical

Hebrew Spelling: רִבְקָה רִבְקָה
(Derived from the Hebrew word "ribqah," which is connected to a root meaning "to tie" or "to bind" — often interpreted as a snare of beauty.)

Pronunciation: reh-BEK-ah

About the Name Rebekah

Rebekah is the biblical spelling of the classic name Rebecca. It is a name of Hebrew origin meaning "to tie" or "to bind." In the Bible, Rebekah is one of the most prominent matriarchs, known as the wife of Isaac and the mother of Jacob and Esau.

Spelling & Variations

While "Rebecca" is the more common spelling in modern times, "Rebekah" is the spelling used in many English translations of the Old Testament. It gives the name a slightly more traditional, vintage, and distinctly biblical feel.

Popularity

Both spellings of the name have enjoyed immense popularity. Rebecca was a top 10 name in the US from the 1970s to the 1990s. The "Rebekah" spelling remains a steady favorite for parents looking for a deeply religious or classic name.

Famous People

Rebekah Vardy is a well-known English television personality. In pop culture, Rebekah Mikaelson is a prominent character in the hit TV series "The Vampire Diaries" and "The Originals."
