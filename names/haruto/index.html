Haruto - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Sunlight flying · Soaring sun

Origin: Japanese

Kanji: 陽 陽
(The kanji 陽 means sun or sunlight, and 翔 means to fly or soar. Together they create the beautiful image of a soaring sun.)

Pronunciation: hah-ROO-toh

About the Name Haruto

Meaning "sunlight flying" or "soaring sun," Haruto has consistently ranked among Japan's most popular boys' names. It projects strength, brightness, and ambition. The imagery associated with the name is incredibly positive, representing a bright future.

Kanji Combinations

Because it has three syllables, Haruto offers many kanji possibilities. The most famous combination is 陽翔 (sun + fly). Another popular variation is 陽斗 (sun + Big Dipper/measure). Parents love mixing the "sun" character (陽) with characters that represent flying, truth, or a person (人).

Popularity

Haruto has been a dominant force in Japanese baby name rankings. It frequently takes the number one spot for boys in Japan. Its popularity is heavily tied to the hope for a bright and successful life for the child.

Famous People

Haruto is a very common name among young Japanese athletes and idols. In pop culture, Haruto Watanabe is a member of the K-pop boy group TREASURE. The name is also frequently used in anime, such as Haruto Soma from Kamen Rider Wizard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Haruto a common name?
Yes, it is one of the most common and popular boys' names in modern Japan.
