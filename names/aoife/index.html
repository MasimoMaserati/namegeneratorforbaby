Aoife - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Beautiful, Radiant, Joyful

Origin: Irish (Gaelic)

Irish Spelling: Aoife Aoife
(Pronounced "EE-fa". The spelling is purely Irish and does not change, though it is sometimes anglicized as Eva or Eve.)

Pronunciation: EE-fa

About the Name Aoife

Meaning "beautiful" or "radiant," Aoife is one of the most iconic names in Irish mythology. It is deeply rooted in Irish culture and has remained a beloved choice for centuries. The name evokes a sense of strength, beauty, and ancient heritage.

Pronunciation & Spelling

The most challenging part of Aoife for non-Irish speakers is the spelling. In the Irish language, the letters "aoi" make an "ee" sound, and "fe" makes an "fa" sound. It is a classic example of how Irish spelling preserves the language's ancient roots.

Popularity

Aoife has consistently ranked among the top 20 most popular girls' names in Ireland for decades. In recent years, its popularity has begun to spread to the UK and North America, as parents look for unique names with a strong cultural identity.

Famous People & Mythology

In Irish mythology, Aoife was a legendary warrior princess and the second wife of the hero Cú Chulainn. In modern times, Aoife Hinds is a well-known Irish actress (famous for her role in "Normal People").
