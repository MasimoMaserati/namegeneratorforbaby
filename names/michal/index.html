Michal - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Who is like God?

Origin: Hebrew / Biblical

Hebrew Spelling: מִיכַל מִיכַל
(A rhetorical question meaning "Who is like God?" It is the feminine form of Michael.)

Pronunciation: mee-KHAL or MEE-khal

About the Name Michal

Michal is a classic Hebrew name meaning "Who is like God?" It is a feminine name closely related to Michael. In the Bible, Michal is a princess, the younger daughter of King Saul, and the first wife of King David. Her story is one of the most complex and fascinating in the Old Testament.

Spelling & Variations

In English, Michal is pronounced "mee-KHAL" (with a guttural 'kh' sound) or simply "MEE-khal." It is important to note that this is distinct from the common male name Michael, despite having the same Hebrew root.

Popularity

Michal is a rare name in the United States and the UK. It is much more common in Israel. Parents who choose it are often drawn to its historical depth and its unique, strong sound that resists trends.

Famous People

While there aren't many modern global celebrities with the name, Michal is an iconic biblical figure. In modern Israel, it is a fairly common name, shared by actresses and public figures.
