Yuna - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Gentleness · Kindness · Moon

Origin: Japanese

Kanji: 優 優
(The kanji 優 means gentleness or kindness, a very popular choice. It can also be written as 結 meaning to bind or tie, or 由 meaning reason.)

Pronunciation: YOO-nah

About the Name Yuna

Yuna combines meanings such as "gentleness," "kindness," and "moon," depending on the kanji used. It has grown in popularity both in Japan and internationally. The name has a soft, melodic sound that feels both modern and deeply rooted in Japanese tradition.

Kanji Combinations

The beauty of Yuna lies in its flexibility. 優 (gentleness) and 奈 (a phonetic character often used in names) is one common combination. Another favorite is 結 (bind/connect) and 菜 (greens), giving the name a feeling of connecting with nature.

Popularity

Yuna has been a staple in the top 50 Japanese girls' names for years. Its international appeal grew significantly thanks to its easy pronunciation for English speakers, making it a favorite among parents looking for a cross-cultural name.

Famous People

Yuna Kim is a world-famous South Korean figure skater (though the name is popular across Asia). Yuna Ito is a well-known Japanese singer and actress. The name is also famously borne by the main character in the popular video game Final Fantasy X.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Yuna mean in Japanese?
Its most common meaning is "gentleness" or "kindness," but it can also mean "moon" or "connect" depending on the characters.
