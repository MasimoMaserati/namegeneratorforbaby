Ezekiel - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: God strengthens

Origin: Hebrew / Biblical

Hebrew Spelling: יְחֶזְקֵאל יְחֶזְקֵאל
(Pronounced Yehezqel in Hebrew. It combines "chazaq" (to strengthen) and "El" (God).)

Pronunciation: ee-ZEE-kee-el

About the Name Ezekiel

Ezekiel is a powerful Hebrew name meaning "God strengthens." It is borne by one of the major prophets in the Old Testament, the author of the Book of Ezekiel. The name conveys a sense of divine strength, resilience, and deep faith.

Spelling & Variations

The name is widely recognized in its English form, Ezekiel. Common nicknames include Zeke, which gives the name a cool, modern, and slightly rugged edge. Other variations include Ezequiel (Spanish) and Hesekiel (German).

Popularity

Ezekiel has seen a steady rise in popularity in the United States over the last two decades. It is frequently ranked in the top 200 names for boys, appealing to parents who want a strong, traditional biblical name that still sounds fresh.

Famous People

Ezekiel Elliott is a very famous NFL running back (often called "Zeke"), making the name highly recognizable in sports. Ezekiel "Zeke" Bratcher is a known character in pop culture, and the name is widely used in literature and film.
