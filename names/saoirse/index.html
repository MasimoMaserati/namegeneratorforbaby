Saoirse - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Freedom, Liberty

Origin: Irish (Gaelic)

Irish Spelling: Saoirse Saoirse
(Pronounced "SEER-sha" or "SUR-sha". It is directly derived from the Irish word for freedom.)

Pronunciation: SEER-sha

About the Name Saoirse

Meaning "freedom" or "liberty," Saoirse is a powerful and deeply meaningful name. It became popular in the 20th century and is closely tied to the Irish struggle for independence. It is a name that represents strength and a free spirit.

Pronunciation & Spelling

The spelling can look intimidating to non-Irish speakers. The "aoi" makes an "ee" sound, and the "rse" makes an "rsha" sound. Once you know the rule, it is quite phonetic. It is sometimes anglicized as Sersha or Sasha, but the traditional spelling is highly preferred.

Popularity

Saoirse is currently one of the most popular names in Ireland, frequently ranking in the top 10. It has also gained significant international attention, largely thanks to the success of the Irish actress Saoirse Ronan.

Famous People

The most famous bearer of the name is Saoirse Ronan, the Academy Award-nominated Irish actress known for her roles in "Lady Bird," "Little Women," and "Brooklyn."
