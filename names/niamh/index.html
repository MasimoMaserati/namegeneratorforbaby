Niamh - Meaning, Origin & Popularity

Meaning: Bright, Radiant

Origin: Irish (Gaelic)

Irish Spelling: Niamh Niamh
(Pronounced "NEEV". The "mh" in Irish makes a "v" sound.)

Pronunciation: NEEV

About the Name Niamh

Meaning "bright" or "radiant," Niamh is a name steeped in Irish mythology. It is one of the most enduring and popular names in Ireland, loved for its soft sound and powerful meaning. It represents a person who brings light and joy.

Pronunciation & Spelling

Niamh is a classic example of Irish phonetics. The combination of "ia" makes an "ee" sound, and the "mh" makes a "v" sound. It is sometimes anglicized as Neve or Nieve, but the traditional spelling is very common and widely recognized.

Popularity

Niamh is incredibly popular in Ireland, consistently ranking in the top 20 names for girls. It has also gained traction in the UK and Australia, and is increasingly recognized in the US.

Famous People & Mythology

In Irish mythology, Niamh was the daughter of the sea god Manannán mac Lir and the queen of the Land of Youth (Tír na nÓg). In modern times, Niamh Kavanagh is a famous Irish singer who won the Eurovision Song Contest.
