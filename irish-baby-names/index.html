50 beautiful Irish baby names
meanings & origins

Irish baby names are admired around the world for their rich history, beautiful sounds, and deep connections to Celtic mythology and Gaelic tradition. Many have been passed down for centuries, inspired by legendary heroes, saints, queens, and Ireland's stunning landscapes. Whether you're searching for a timeless classic or a unique name with ancient roots, these Irish names offer plenty of inspiration.

✨ Girls' Irish baby names

Aoife

EE-fa
Beauty · Radiance

Aoife is one of the best-known names in Irish mythology. A fearless warrior princess, Aoife symbolizes courage, strength, and beauty. Today, it remains among Ireland's most popular girls' names.

⚔️ mythological warrior

Saoirse

SEER-sha

A modern favorite that symbolizes independence and hope. Saoirse has risen in popularity worldwide, partly thanks to Irish actress Saoirse Ronan, and carries a powerful sense of liberty.

NEEV

Maeve

🔊 A note on pronunciation

Irish names often use spellings that are unfamiliar to English speakers because they follow Irish Gaelic pronunciation rules. Names like Aoife, Saoirse, Caoimhe, Niamh, and Tadhg may look difficult at first, but once learned they become some of the most distinctive and memorable names in the world.

If pronunciation is important to you, try saying the names aloud or listen to native Irish speakers online before making your final choice.
