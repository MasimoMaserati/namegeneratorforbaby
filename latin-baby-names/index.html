Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a name from this collection?

Say each name aloud with your surname, consider spelling and pronunciation in the languages your family uses, and check that its cultural background feels right for you.

Are the meanings definitive?

Not always. Name etymologies can be debated and may vary by spelling, language and historical period. Treat brief notes as a starting point and verify details with reliable language or cultural sources.

Can a name have more than one origin?

Yes. The same spelling may appear in several languages with different histories and meanings. The relevant origin depends on the specific family and cultural context.
